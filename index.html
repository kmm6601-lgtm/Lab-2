What I learned

After searching for this new wild cat discovery, I learned from an article that it is called a tilcayo tiger cat and that it is located in the Bolivian cloud forest, which is along the Yungas region where the Andes meet the Amazon basin. For the first time in a century, researchers identified a new cat species! When you hear things like that, it's very fascinating and attention-grabbing because, even though we have been here so long, it's a reminder that we still have yet to scratch the surface of everything the world has to offer. Since it's such a new species, researchers are still unclear about how big the population of the tilcayo tiger cat is. However, they did note that the habitats where they were found are quickly vanishing.

My Final Thoughts

These tilcayo tiger cats are extremely small and described as kitten-like, which is actually how the discovery was originally made. A man had brought the wild cat into a sanctuary because he could no longer take care of it, thinking it was a regular domesticated cat. Nogales-Ascarrunz, a volunteer at that very sanctuary discovered through genetic testing that it was actually a new species that “diverged from other tiger cat lineages about 1.4 million years ago”. Ultimately, there may be more undiscovered species at risk, and, as she emphasized, conservationists play a crucial role in ensuring vulnerable animals are protected rather than lost forever without ever being recognized.
